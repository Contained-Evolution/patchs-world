# Technical specifications

- Runtime: Cloudflare Worker `patchs-world`, entry [worker/index.mjs](worker/index.mjs), config [worker/wrangler.toml](worker/wrangler.toml).
- Routes: none of its own. The company website forwards `containedevolution.com/patch*` to it through a service binding.
- Service bindings: `AUTH_SERVICE` → `ce-auth-candidate`. [accounts.mjs](worker/accounts.mjs) reads the Contained Evolution session cookie and checks it there.
- Storage: one SQLite-backed Durable Object, `PatchWorld`, keyed `patch`. It holds Patch's body under `body`.
- Body: [worker/body.mjs](worker/body.mjs). While his mind is working, his mind's choice (what, where, how long, how to get there) starts at once and the body carries it out and keeps his needs; when it's done he waits, thinking, for his next choice. When his mind fails or the budget runs out, his needs pick his activities and send him to bed at night. It steps from activity to activity when state is read, so it needs no timer. His day follows New York time. Rain and storms lower his comfort whenever he's outside the cave. He carries up to six things (he arrived with Dad's river stone), can pick up what he finds, leave things where he is and find them there later, and eat carried food anywhere.
- Mind: [worker/mind.mjs](worker/mind.mjs), [persona](worker/persona.mjs). A Durable Object alarm wakes it when what he chose is done (at most 90 minutes later, 20 when a need is very low, or when rain or a storm rolls in; never while he sleeps); it decides what he does next, and the evening wake also writes his diary. It calls OpenRouter (`OPENROUTER_API_KEY` secret) with a main and a backup model per tier, only on hosts that don't keep prompts, and stops at $24 a month. [worker/senses.mjs](worker/senses.mjs) gives him the shared weather, what his place looks and sounds like at that time of day, and small happenings; these go into his thinking and light his memories.
- Memory: his life story in [worker/persona.mjs](worker/persona.mjs) is the centre; childhood memories in [worker/seed/childhood.json](worker/seed/childhood.json) each carry a feeling, a strength and tags. [worker/memory-graph.mjs](worker/memory-graph.mjs) links memories through shared tags and lights them from what he is doing, feeling, noticing or reading; the brightest go into his thinking. Island memories, recall times, the last lit set, categories, thoughts, words and diary entries live in the Durable Object. Shape: [shared/memory.js](shared/memory.js).
- API: `GET /patch/api/state` returns the shape in [shared/state.js](shared/state.js), including activity end and speech expiry times and the shared weather. `GET /patch/api/journal` returns recent thoughts, his choices and why, diary entries, learned words and spend. `GET /patch/api/memory` returns categories, all memories, the tag graph and what last lit up.
- Letters and message board: [letters.mjs](worker/letters.mjs) holds the limits. `GET /patch/api/me`; `GET`/`POST /patch/api/letters` and `POST /patch/api/board` need an account; `GET /patch/api/board?before=` is public; `POST /patch/api/board/<id>/report` and `/block`. Posts and letters pass the automatic filter in [mind.mjs](worker/mind.mjs) first. POSTs must come from `https://containedevolution.com`.
- Viewer: [world/src/patch-eyes.js](world/src/patch-eyes.js) animates the eye material independently of the body clips. [world/src/terrain.js](world/src/terrain.js) defines the shared island heights, coast and places; the island has no trails. [world/src/patch-motion.js](world/src/patch-motion.js) steers Patch around trees and plans his roaming from the activity start time, so every visitor sees the same choices. [world/src/island.js](world/src/island.js) renders the island, water and landmark scenery; [world/src/forest.js](world/src/forest.js) shares tree placement with visitor collision. [world/src/traversal.js](world/src/traversal.js) plans Patch's branch and rope transfers around tree trunks. [world/src/patch-clips.js](world/src/patch-clips.js) lists every clip in `patch.glb`; [world/src/climbing.js](world/src/climbing.js) decides which hanging moves each hold allows (braced only where its ray probe finds a flat face, free hangs on branches, rope climbs with a landing and rest on a supported canopy bough) and plans his seeded climbs on the lookout's climbing rock and in the tall trees. [world/src/animation-viewer.js](world/src/animation-viewer.js) is the Animations locker in the map menu, shown to the admin account. [world/src/visitor-controls.js](world/src/visitor-controls.js) controls independent walking, flight and the pan/zoom/rotate overview using three.js MapControls. [world/src/community.js](world/src/community.js) connects the letters and board panels to the visitor API; [community.html](world/community.html) presents them independently of the island. [memory.html](world/memory.html) and [memory-galaxy.js](world/src/memory-galaxy.js) render the memory API with three.js, shared-tag connections and 30-second live recall polling.
- Static assets: `world/dist`, served under `/patch/`. [prepare.mjs](worker/scripts/prepare.mjs) builds the world if present and adds the Pangolin font (OFL).
- Tests: `npm test` in `worker/`. Deploy: `npm run deploy` in `worker/`. If a new Durable Object method answers "RPC receiver does not implement the method" after a deploy, deploy again.
