# Technical specifications

- Runtime: Cloudflare Worker `patchs-world`, entry [worker/index.mjs](worker/index.mjs), config [worker/wrangler.toml](worker/wrangler.toml).
- Routes: its own address, `patch.containedevolution.com` (a custom domain in [wrangler.toml](worker/wrangler.toml)), so it installs and runs as its own app apart from Contained Evolution's. The company website still forwards `containedevolution.com/patch*` to it through a service binding; those requests move to the new address, and `/patch/sw.js` there is a service worker that clears copies saved at the old address.
- Service bindings: `AUTH_SERVICE` → `ce-auth-candidate`. [accounts.mjs](worker/accounts.mjs) reads the Contained Evolution session cookie and checks it there.
- Storage: one SQLite-backed Durable Object, `PatchWorld`, keyed `patch`. It holds Patch's body under `body`, his mind, memories, letters, board posts, journal entries (`diary:<date>T<time>`) and each account's notification devices (`push:<account id>`).
- Body: [worker/body.mjs](worker/body.mjs). While his mind is working, his mind's choice (what, where, how long, how to get there) starts at once and the body carries it out and keeps his needs; when it's done he waits, thinking, for his next choice. When his mind fails or the budget runs out, his needs pick his activities and send him to bed at night. It steps from activity to activity when state is read, so it needs no timer. His day follows New York time. Rain and storms lower his comfort whenever he's outside the cave. He carries up to six things (he arrived with Dad's river stone), can pick up what he finds, leave things where he is and find them there later, and eat carried food anywhere.
- Mind: [worker/mind.mjs](worker/mind.mjs), [persona](worker/persona.mjs). A Durable Object alarm wakes it when what he chose is done (at most 90 minutes later, 20 when a need is very low, or when rain or a storm rolls in; never while he sleeps); it decides what he does next, and the evening wake also writes his diary. It calls OpenRouter (`OPENROUTER_API_KEY` secret) with a main and a backup model per tier, only on hosts that don't keep prompts, and stops at $24 a month. [worker/senses.mjs](worker/senses.mjs) gives him the shared weather, what his place looks and sounds like at that time of day, and small happenings; these go into his thinking and light his memories.
- Coconut computer and tablet: the coconut computer is the `computer` place at camp, the only place he reads and answers letters. His tablet goes everywhere: `research` searches the web there or at the computer, `write` lets him write journal entries, add memories and rewrite or move his island memories (childhood memories stay fixed). A new letter pings him: the alarm moves up to two minutes away (at most every 20 minutes, never while he sleeps) and his notes say mail is waiting. Searches use OpenRouter’s Exa web plugin. The Durable Object reserves spend before each search against the $25 New York calendar-day cap and reconciles the search and page-check charges from OpenRouter usage. An unconfirmed charge keeps its reservation. Patch receives an internet warning at 10% remaining. Adult and gore sites are excluded. He closes any page that is inappropriate while reading, a page check closes any he missed, and he carries on with the rest of the results; nothing else is blocked for later searches.
- Memory: his life story in [worker/persona.mjs](worker/persona.mjs) is the centre; childhood memories in [worker/seed/childhood.json](worker/seed/childhood.json) each carry a feeling, a strength and tags. [worker/memory-graph.mjs](worker/memory-graph.mjs) links memories through shared tags and lights them from what he is doing, feeling, noticing or reading; the brightest go into his thinking. Island memories, recall times, the last lit set, categories, thoughts, words and diary entries live in the Durable Object. Shape: [shared/memory.js](shared/memory.js).
- API: `GET /api/state` returns the shape in [shared/state.js](shared/state.js), including activity end and speech expiry times, the shared weather and a short-lived gift sound cue. `GET /api/journal` returns tablet and evening entries, search notes and sources, internet availability and learned words for [the journal page](world/journal.html). `GET /api/memory` returns categories, all memories, the tag graph and what last lit up.
- Accounts: people sign in and create accounts on `https://containedevolution.com/`, opened in its own window (on an iPhone Home Screen app, in the app's browser sheet, which keeps the app's own sign-in); panels check the account again when the page is shown. Patch knows people by their Contained Evolution gamer tag.
- Letters and message board: [letters.mjs](worker/letters.mjs) holds the limits. `GET /api/me`; `GET`/`POST /api/letters` and `POST /api/board` need an account; `GET /api/board?before=` is public; `POST /api/board/<id>/report` and `/block`. Lookup suggestions use the letter API and enter Patch’s research notes when he accepts them. Posts and letters pass the automatic filter in [mind.mjs](worker/mind.mjs) first. POSTs must come from `https://patch.containedevolution.com`.
- Gifts: [gifts.mjs](worker/gifts.mjs) defines Patch’s gift actions and ball wear. Signed-in accounts use `GET`/`POST` `/api/gifts` for their [camp drop-off](world/gifts.html). The Durable Object stores gift ownership and Patch’s choices.
- Viewer: [world/src/patch-eyes.js](world/src/patch-eyes.js) and [world/src/patch-mouth.js](world/src/patch-mouth.js) animate the face independently of the body clips; mouth movement follows the server speech window. [world/src/terrain.js](world/src/terrain.js) defines the shared island heights, coast and places; the island has no trails. [world/src/patch-motion.js](world/src/patch-motion.js) steers Patch around trees and plans his roaming from the activity start time, so every visitor sees the same choices. [world/src/island.js](world/src/island.js) renders the island, water and landmark scenery; [world/src/forest.js](world/src/forest.js) places trees across the island. [world/src/traversal.js](world/src/traversal.js) plans Patch's branch and rope transfers around tree trunks. [world/src/patch-clips.js](world/src/patch-clips.js) lists every clip in `patch.glb`; [world/src/climbing.js](world/src/climbing.js) decides which hanging moves each hold allows (braced only where its ray probe finds a flat face, free hangs on branches, rope climbs with a landing and rest on a supported canopy bough followed by a rope descent to the ground) and plans his seeded climbs on the lookout's climbing rock and in the tall trees. [world/src/furniture.js](world/src/furniture.js) holds the seats, the coconut computer desk sequence and when he holds his tablet; [world/src/camp-furniture.js](world/src/camp-furniture.js) builds the coconut computer, chairs, bench, table and stool. [world/src/animation-viewer.js](world/src/animation-viewer.js) is the Animations locker in the map menu, shown to the admin account. [world/src/visitor-controls.js](world/src/visitor-controls.js) controls independent walking and the Patch tracking view. [world/src/island-cameras.js](world/src/island-cameras.js) renders fixed camera feeds from the shared island scene and renderer. [world/src/community.js](world/src/community.js) connects the letters and board panels to the visitor API; [community.html](world/community.html) presents them independently of the island. [memory.html](world/memory.html) and [memory-galaxy.js](world/src/memory-galaxy.js) render the memory API with three.js, shared-tag connections and 30-second live recall polling.
- Walk collision: [visitor-controls.js](world/src/visitor-controls.js) and [patch-motion.js](world/src/patch-motion.js) use the shared ground and interaction rules in [island-collision.js](world/src/island-collision.js). [hammock.js](world/src/hammock.js) aligns the fabric and sleeping poses.
- Sound: [island-audio.js](world/src/island-audio.js) uses Web Audio for the active view’s ambience, footsteps and Patch’s activity cues. The visitor’s mute setting is stored locally.
- Foraging: [foraging.js](world/src/foraging.js) uses the same banana trees as the renderer and schedules shared collection and storage animations; [food-props.js](world/src/food-props.js) renders the carried basket and hinged store. These props are visual; the body inventory remains the Worker’s carried-item list.
- Notifications: [push.mjs](worker/push.mjs) sends Web Push (aes128gcm, VAPID with the `VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY` var and `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY` secret) only to browser push services. `GET`/`POST /api/push` and `POST /api/push/remove` need an account; each device chooses replies, journal entries or both, up to five devices an account. After his mind runs, a reply goes to its writer's devices and a journal or diary entry to every device that asked; push services that answer 404 or 410 drop the device. The menu's Notifications panel is [notifications.js](world/src/notifications.js); a reply notification opens `/?view=letters` and a journal one opens the journal page.
- App: [manifest.webmanifest](world/manifest.webmanifest) installs `patch.containedevolution.com` as Patch's World; [tools/make-icons.mjs](tools/make-icons.mjs) renders its icons from `patch.glb`. [sw.js](world/sw.js) caches every built file except source maps under one version per build, copies unchanged files from the last version, never caches `/api/`, and shows notifications. [app.js](world/src/app.js) registers it and shows an update button when a new version is waiting. [install.js](world/src/install.js) shows the "Get the Patch app" button outside the installed app: the browser's install prompt where it offers one, otherwise that browser's install steps.
- Static assets: `world/dist`, served at the root. [prepare.mjs](worker/scripts/prepare.mjs) builds the world if present and adds the Pangolin font (OFL).
- Tests: `npm test` in `world/` and `worker/`. Deploy: `npm run deploy` in `worker/`. If a new Durable Object method answers "RPC receiver does not implement the method" after a deploy, deploy again.
